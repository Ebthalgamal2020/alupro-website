About ALUPRO

Built With Precision. Designed for Living.

ALUPRO Aluminium Solutions works across architectural aluminium and glass — windows, doors, sliding systems, façades, glass rooms and pergolas, from the first measurement to the finished installation.

Who We Are

An Aluminium Solutions Company.

ALUPRO works across architectural aluminium and glass — from a single window opening to a full glazed elevation. The work covers windows and doors, sliding systems, glass rooms, pergolas, façades, shutters and custom fabrication, delivered for residential and commercial projects alike.

Aluminium is an unforgiving material to work in. A frame that is a few millimetres out of square will show, and no amount of finishing hides it afterwards. That is why the approach below runs the same way on every project, whatever its size.

We work with architects, contractors and private clients — reading the drawing, agreeing the detail, and taking responsibility for the result through to handover.

Engineered Detail

Systems specified and detailed for the opening they actually sit in.

Controlled Fabrication

Cutting, machining and assembly held to consistent tolerances.

Clean Installation

On-site work planned, sequenced and finished without shortcuts.

Our Approach

Four Steps, Followed on Every Project.

  1. 01

    Understand the Space

    Every project begins with understanding how the space will be used — the opening, the light, the way people move through it and what the finished room has to do.

  2. 02

    Define the Solution

    Selecting the right aluminium and architectural system for the project, then setting out the profiles, glazing and finishes that suit it.

  3. 03

    Precision in Detail

    Careful attention to proportions, framing, finishes and integration, so the system sits in the building rather than on top of it.

  4. 04

    From Fabrication to Installation

    A complete process from the chosen solution through to final implementation, with the same team carrying the detail from workshop to site.

Quality

The Difference Is in the Detail.

A clean line is not an accident. It comes from proportions that were decided early, frames that were set out to the same grid, and junctions that were drawn before they were built.

Where aluminium meets glass, meets stone, meets the floor — those are the points that decide whether a space reads as considered or merely finished.

  • Clean lines held across the elevation
  • Proportions decided before fabrication
  • Aluminium and glass detailed as one system
  • Attention carried through to the finished result
