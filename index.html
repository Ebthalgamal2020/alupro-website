Who We Are

Aluminium Solutions Built Around Your Vision.

ALUPRO is an aluminium solutions company working across architectural systems — from windows and doors to full curtain wall façades, glass systems and outdoor structures. We work with architects, contractors and private clients to turn drawings into accurately fabricated, cleanly installed aluminium work.

Every project runs through the same discipline: understand the requirement, engineer the detail, fabricate to specification, and install with care. The result is aluminium that fits properly, operates smoothly and holds its finish.

How We Work

From First Drawing to Final Handover.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We start with the requirement — the opening, the use, the look you are after and the constraints around it. Site conditions and drawings are reviewed before anything is quoted.

  2. 02

    Design & Planning

    System selection, profiles, glazing and finishes are set out and detailed, then confirmed with you so there are no surprises once fabrication begins.

  3. 03

    Fabrication

    Profiles are cut, machined and assembled to the agreed specification, with each unit checked against the drawing before it leaves the workshop.

  4. 04

    Installation

    Installation is sequenced around the site programme, with fixing, sealing and alignment handled by the team that knows the system.

  5. 05

    Final Delivery

    Operation is tested, finishes are cleaned down and the work is walked through with you before it is signed off as complete.
