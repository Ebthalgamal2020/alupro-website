Solutions Built Around Your Space.

From large architectural openings to refined outdoor systems, ALUPRO creates aluminium and glass solutions designed around the way each space is used.

Sliding Doors & Windows

Large openings that let a room borrow the light and the view outside it. The frame is kept as narrow as the span allows, so what you see is glass and the line of the aluminium holding it.

  • Wide openings that connect inside and outside directly
  • Slim aluminium framing kept consistent across the elevation
  • Daylight brought deep into the room without losing the view
  • Sizes and configurations set out for the opening in question

Glass Rooms

An enclosed room made mostly of glass — a garden room, a terrace enclosure, an extension to a living space. It adds usable floor area while keeping the connection to the garden intact.

  • Living space extended without closing the outside off
  • Daylight from every side of the enclosure
  • Modern glazing carried on a quiet aluminium frame
  • A comfortable, sheltered room in use through the year

Pergolas

A structure that makes the outside of the house usable. Aluminium holds a long span on a slim section, so the shade sits where it is needed without the frame taking over the terrace.

  • Outdoor living given a defined, sheltered area
  • Shade controlled across the day
  • A modern structure with a clear architectural line
  • Set out to sit correctly against the building it serves

Architectural Façades

The glazed face of a building, assembled as one system. Framing, glass and fixings are detailed together so the grid stays true across every floor and the elevation reads as a single surface.

  • Large glazed surfaces carried as one continuous system
  • Aluminium framing aligned across the full elevation
  • A clean, modern exterior expression
  • External systems detailed for the building they clad
